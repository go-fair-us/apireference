### Common Themes in These Diabetes-Related Clinical Trials

These trials primarily focus on diabetes management, treatment optimization, and risk reduction across diverse populations. Here are the most prominent **common themes**, supported by examples:

1. **Glycemic Control as a Core Outcome**
   Nearly all trials measure improvements in HbA1c, blood glucose (fasting, postprandial, nocturnal), or normoglycemia regression.
   - *Examples*: NCT00174668 (HbA1c change with intensified insulin); NCT04873050 (semaglutide for postpartum normoglycemia); NCT05286892 (glycemic control via app).

2. **Pharmacological Interventions and Comparative Efficacy**
   A majority test insulin analogs, GLP-1/GIP agonists, SGLT2 inhibitors, or other antidiabetics for superiority, safety, or novel applications.
   - *Examples*: NCT00174668/NCT01620450 (insulin regimens/comparisons); NCT04873050/NCT02759107 (semaglutide/tirzepatide); NCT07272057 (SGLT2 in cancer); NCT01574820 (rosiglitazone in pre-DM).

3. **Safety, Tolerability, and Adverse Events**
   Emphasis on hypoglycemia, cardiovascular toxicity, side effects, pharmacokinetics (PK), and pharmacodynamics (PD), especially in vulnerable groups.
   - *Examples*: NCT00174668 (hypoglycemia, AEs); NCT02759107 (tirzepatide safety/PK/PD); NCT07272057 (CV toxicity in cancer patients).

4. **Special Populations and Comorbidities**
   Trials target non-general populations, such as those with comorbidities (e.g., cancer, CAD, postpartum GDM) or settings (e.g., hospital, students).
   - *Examples*: NCT04873050 (postpartum GDM); NCT07272057 (lung cancer + T2D); NCT01574820 (pre-DM + CAD); NCT05286450 (diabetic students); NCT01154413 (hospital non-ICU).

5. **Patient-Centered Outcomes and Adherence**
   Focus on real-world factors like compliance, self-care, quality of life, distress, and non-drug interventions (e.g., protocols, apps, exercise).
