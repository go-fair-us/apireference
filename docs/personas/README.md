# Personas 


## About

So this is just a placeholder.  As we work to define the audience, it might be useful
to define personas that represent the different types of users that will be using the products.

## Example Personas

- **Researcher**: A scientist or researcher who is looking for specific data or information to support their work.
- **Data Analyst**: A professional who uses data to make informed decisions and drive business strategies.
- **End User**: A person who uses the product for personal or professional purposes without necessarily understanding the underlying technology.

## Benefits of Personas

Using personas can help us design products that better meet the needs and expectations of our target audience. By understanding the motivations, behaviors, and pain points of different user types, we can create more effective and user-friendly products.
